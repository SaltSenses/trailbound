Map legend, sources & limitations

Numbered green clusters open a list of every indexed hike in the group. Green lines preview routes in the viewed area; orange shows the selected route; purple pins are destinations; blue pins are staging candidates. Zoom to see route previews (up to 120 at a time). General map paths are not necessarily verified hiking routes. 3D uses real elevation data with 1.2× relief exaggeration. Basemap and terrain require internet and WebGL. Drag with two fingers or use the compass to rotate; pinch to zoom.

Basemap: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors. Terrain: Mapterhorn and its elevation sources. Map providers receive requests for the area you view. Location is requested only when you choose the location control.
